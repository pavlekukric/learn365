## Suspected facts (not changed)

- 155 — „ulazili u muslimansku versku zajednicu — milet —”: Muslims were the ruling community, not a millet in the Ottoman sense; also „bošnjačko-muslimansko ime” is anachronistic for the 15th–17th c.
- 158 — „Dana 12. oktobra 1579.”: most references give 11 October 1579 for Sokolović's assassination.
- 170 — „despot Stefan Lazarević držao je posede u južnoj Ugarskoj — Beograd, …”: Belgrade was not in southern Hungary; he received it from the Hungarian king (wording conflates).
- 171 — subtitle „Vek i po sukoba” / body „Tokom skoro sto pedeset godina”, „u nepunih sto pedeset godina”: the wars covered are 1683–1791 (~110 years).
- 172 — „u gradu koji je još pre dve decenije bio osmanska palanka”: Ottoman Belgrade was a major city/fortress, not a „palanka”.
- 175 — „Bečki kongres je 1814. svu Boku predao Habzburškoj monarhiji”: Austria took Boka in 1814; the Congress of Vienna's Final Act is 1815.
- 176 — „iz Dubrovnika su u zaleđe stizali … od 17. veka i kafa”: coffee reached the Balkans via the Ottoman east, not from Dubrovnik.
- 178 vs 180 — 178: Suvorov opened the „Slavjanska škola” in 1726, „iz nje će se razviti … Slaveno-latinska škola”; 180: „Karlovačka latinska škola, osnovana 1726, a od 1733. pod Emanuilom Kozačinskim” — the Latin school is usually dated 1733 (Kozačinski); the two lessons conflict.
- 180 — „Veliki ikonostas manastira Krušedola, dovršen u poslednjoj trećini 18. veka”: the Krušedol iconostasis is generally dated to the 1750s.
- 187 — Koča „odveden je u obližnju Tekiju … pogubljen”: several sources place the impalement at Kladovo (Fetislam); also captured „braneći Brzasku u Banatu” in Sept 1788 — worth a check.
- 189 vs 188 — 189: janissaries „oterani 1791”; 188 implies expulsion under Hadži Mustafa-paša after 1793 (usually 1791–1792 firman/1792 expulsion). Minor inconsistency.
- 190 — „Krajem januara po starom kalendaru, oko Svetog Save”: Sveti Sava is 14 January OS (= late January NS); the calendar qualifier looks wrong.
